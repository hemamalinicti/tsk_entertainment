Terms & Conditions

Clear, transparent, and fair standards that guarantee the highest quality execution for every celebration we undertake across Tamil Nadu.

Effective Date: October 2026 • TSK Entertainments (Tamil Nadu, India)

1. Scope of Services

TSK Entertainments provides professional full-service event planning, stage and floral décor, sound and lighting engineering, celebrity artist booking, hospitality coordination, and on-ground show execution across Tamil Nadu.

2. Booking & Date Confirmation

An event date is officially reserved upon mutual agreement of scope and receipt of an initial advance booking deposit. Due to heavy demand during auspicious muhurtham dates and festival seasons, tentative dates without deposit cannot be held indefinitely.

3. Quotations & Scope Adjustments

All quotations provided are detailed and itemized based on client specifications. Any significant additions requested closer to the event (e.g. extra artist performances, extended venue lighting, or expanded floral installations) will be documented with transparent supplementary costings.

4. Celebrity Management & Artist Logistics

For celebrity and musical artist appearances, TSK Entertainments handles all contractual riders, technical stage requirements, green room setup, and local security. Artist performance schedules are governed by artist availability and venue time restrictions.

5. Venue Regulations & Permissions

Events must comply with local government guidelines, municipal decibel limits, fire safety standards, and venue house rules. We work alongside venue management to ensure all safety certifications and setups are completely compliant.

6. Rescheduling & Cancellations

In the event of unforeseen rescheduling or force majeure (e.g. severe weather, natural disruptions), we work closely with families and organizers to transfer services to an alternate available date with minimal disruption.

7. Governing Jurisdiction

All service agreements and operations are governed by the applicable laws of the State of Tamil Nadu, India.
