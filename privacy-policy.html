Your trust and privacy are paramount. We treat every celebration, family milestone, and client interaction with utmost confidentiality and care.

Effective Date: October 2026 • TSK Entertainments (Tamil Nadu, India)

1. Introduction

TSK Entertainments (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you visit our website or engage our event planning and celebrity management services across Tamil Nadu.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information necessary to plan, produce, and execute your celebrations:

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, WhatsApp contact, and email address.
  • Event Details: Celebration date, event category (wedding, sangeet, birthday, corporate event, etc.), venue, city in Tamil Nadu, and approximate guest count.
  • Planning Preferences: Themes, stage décor vision, musical and artist preferences, and special logistical requests.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Prepare customized proposals, stage visual mockups, and transparent itemized quotations.
  • Coordinate schedules with artists, vendors, venue managers, and audio-visual technicians.
  • Communicate directly with you via phone call, WhatsApp, or email regarding event updates.
  • Ensure flawless, punctual, and safe on-ground event management.

4. WhatsApp & Direct Communication

When you click our WhatsApp links or submit our “Plan My Event” form, you initiate an opt-in direct chat with our senior event producer (+91 6383180073). We will never send unsolicited marketing spam or share your contact number with third-party telemarketers.

5. Event Photography & Client Privacy

We deeply respect the sacred privacy of personal family ceremonies and confidential VIP corporate gatherings. Photographs and videos captured during events are only featured in our portfolio or social media (@tskentertainments) with prior mutual consent from the host family or organization.

6. Data Protection & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Relevant event details are shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with trusted production partners (e.g. florist teams, caterers, stage carpenters, and sound engineers) solely for executing your specific booking.
